Examples of things to ask Dex:
==============================

/**** MTG RULES ****/

1. Explain the difference between "Protection from color" and "Hexproof from color"
2. Ask about the spell stack and casting speed
3. Ask about first strike and double strike
4. Ask about deathstrike, and how that works with first strike or double strike

/**** CARDS ****/

1. I love Magic for its classic artwork. What's an older card that just screams Magic: The Gathering?
2. Is it Enchanting Wilds... or Evolving Wilds? I can't remember - that one card that lets you search your library for a land card.
3. Ask to show me a random card - maybe a cult classic or less-popular but fun one
    - The first time I asked this I got Atog, which yielded lots of results and started a fun conversation about Commander

/**** CULTURE ****/

1. Ask about banding and why it doesn't exist anymore
    - Note that his explanation actually helped me understand banding more lol
2. Ask about the popular formats

Demo Notes:
============

Some notes for a live, or recorded session with Dex. I have tested the below cases and get good responses from them. There's always room for tuning, but this is a great demo.

1. Mono White Toxic

- Demonstrate that he can read my deck and give me an idea of what it does
- Ask about Skrelv ruling
- Ask if there are any similar decks out there

2. Green Creatures

- Demonstrate that he can find weaknesses in my deck
- Talk through a replacement for Joraga

3. In-Progress Gobbies

- Ask to review, reading oracle text for key card(s) understanding that it is WIP and not legal
- Tell him you're working on Historic, and ask for direction.
- Specifically ask for instants and sorcs that could help

4. Izzet

- Demonstrate deep knowledge during review
- Ask about artifact sacrifice engines, reference Ixalan
- Note for the audience that he suggested a card that I actually put in my deck
